Delete your YojanaSahay account

YojanaSahay (Android app and website, developer: Sahnawaz Ahmed Laskar) lets you delete your account and its data at any time.

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

  1. Open YojanaSahay and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Without the app

E-mail us from the e-mail address (or tell us the mobile number) you signed in with:

E-mail a deletion request

Subject: "Data Deletion Request". We complete it within 7 working days and confirm by e-mail.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (name, e-mail or mobile number, profile photo).
  • Your saved eligibility profile and answers.
  • Your tracked applications (My Applications).
  • Your phone-notification settings and e-mail reminders.

What may be kept

  • Problem reports you sent (for example a broken scheme link) are kept without your name, e-mail or phone, so the fix is not lost.
  • Anonymous usage counts, which never contained your identity.

Chat history and family members are stored only on your phone — signing out or uninstalling the app removes them.
